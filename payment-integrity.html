What Gets Caught

The things a rules engine
can't find on its own.

Deterministic rules catch what they're written to catch. The AI layer catches what rules miss — clinical reasoning failures, cross-signal fraud patterns, and sequencing issues that only a trained coder or physician would normally flag.

NCCI bundling violations
Component procedures billed alongside the parent — with the exact CMS policy chapter cited.
DRG tier upcoding
MCC/CC diagnoses with POA=N excluded from tier calculation per HAC reduction policy.
COB payer order errors
Birthday rule, Medicare secondary, Medicaid last resort — resolved before any financial adjudication.
Diagnosis-procedure mismatch
Clinical reasoning catches right knee diagnosis billed with right hip procedure — something no NCCI table covers.
Inappropriate inpatient admission
LOS below DRG geometric mean triggers 2-midnight rule review. Short stays flagged for observation reclassification.
Above-contract billing
Billed charges compared to contracted rate with modifier adjustments — bilateral, MPR, assistant surgeon, global surgery.
Provider identity gaps
Group NPI as rendering NPI + missing TIN = potential non-contracted provider billing pattern. Flagged for SIU.
FWA outlier patterns
Provider billing 340% above peer group median. Systematic unbundling across multiple claims. Referred to FWA team.
Duplicate and near-duplicate claims
Same-day same-service intra-claim duplicates flagged immediately. Cross-claim history match in production.

Professional claims route through CPT editing. Institutional claims route through DRG validation — MS-DRG tier accuracy, CC/MCC documentation, HAC exclusions, LOS outliers, and discharge status. Both tracks output the same structured decision.

How It Deploys

One endpoint.
No rip and replace.

Before your adjudication system pays, it calls one REST endpoint. We return approve, pend, or deny — with a cited policy basis — in under 60 seconds. Plugs into Facets, QNXT, HealthEdge, or any core admin system without touching your existing workflow.

  • FHIR R4 JSON or X12 837P/I input — both supported natively
  • Every finding includes rule ID, policy citation, and suggested action
  • Audit trail built for provider appeals from day one
  • LLM provider is client-configurable — Azure, Anthropic, or on-premises Ollama
  • Deploy on AWS, your own cloud, or Railway — Dockerfile included
