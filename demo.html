Intelligence layer
Every stage scores every claim.
Three dimensions.
Not a rules engine that fires or doesn't. A scoring platform that quantifies certainty, risk, and data completeness — then routes automatically based on thresholds your team configures.
Confidence Score
91%
How certain is the system? Rule-based stages reach 97%+. AI stages range 75–95% depending on data completeness. Below 75% → always human review.
Risk Score
72/100
Financial + compliance risk. Driven by billed amount, finding severity, provider history, FWA signals. ≥80 → SIU auto-referral. ≥60 → auto-deny. ≥40 → pend.
Data Quality
95%
How complete is the input? Missing NPI, stale eligibility, incomplete diagnoses all reduce this score. Below 70% → never auto-execute — always human review.
Your team configures the thresholds. A conservative Medicaid MCO sets auto-deny at risk 40. An aggressive commercial plan sets it at 70. Same platform, same pipeline, different risk tolerance. Every threshold change is audited with who changed it and when.
